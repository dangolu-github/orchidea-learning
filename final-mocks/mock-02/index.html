Reading Task 2 — Multiple matching

Four community courses are described below. Choose A, B, C or D for each person R11–R20. A course may be used more than once.

A — Practical Photography
This six-week Saturday course is for people who already know the basic controls on their camera but want stronger composition. Most sessions take place outdoors, including one early-morning landscape trip. Learners must bring a camera; phones are not suitable. The fee includes printing your best two images for a final exhibition.

B — Cooking from the Cupboard
Learn to create affordable meals from ingredients that are easy to store. No previous cooking experience is needed. The Wednesday evening class provides all ingredients and equipment, and participants eat together afterwards. Vegetarian choices are always available, but the kitchen cannot guarantee a completely nut-free space.

C — Speak with Confidence
This friendly Monday workshop helps adults who feel nervous when speaking to a group. Through short games, pair work and two-minute talks, participants practise organising ideas and using their voice clearly. There is no written homework. The course ends with an optional presentation to invited friends.

D — Fix Your Bicycle
Bring your own bicycle to the sports hall on Sunday afternoons. A mechanic will teach you to check brakes, repair a tyre and make simple adjustments. Tools are supplied, and covered bicycle parking is available. The course is not suitable for electric bicycles or for anyone under sixteen.
