1Reading · After-School Notices

阅读 A–D 四则通知。问题 1–6 分别对应哪一则通知？每题写一个字母；同一个字母可以使用多次。

A · Science Museum Visit

The museum visit is on Saturday. We first advertised an 8:30 departure, but the coach company has asked us to leave at 8:10. Meet at the school’s north gate at 7:55. The ticket costs €7 and must be paid to Ms Horváth by Wednesday. Lunch is not included, so bring food and water.

B · Homework Help

Need help with Maths or English? Come to the library on any Tuesday between 3:30 and 5:00. No booking is needed. Bring a task you have already tried and mark the exact step that is confusing. Tutors will not complete the whole homework for you. Laptops are available, but you must use your school account.

C · Drama Weekend

Friday’s rehearsal has moved from the main hall to Room 12 because the stage lights are being repaired. It starts at 4:00 and ends at 6:00. Saturday’s performance is still in the main hall. Actors should wear black clothes; costumes will be supplied. Parents may enter free, but they must reserve a seat online.

D · Bike Care Class

The workshop begins at 4:15 on Thursday. There are only six places, so message Mr Nagy before Wednesday. Bring your bike and a lock; all repair tools and gloves will be supplied. If it rains, the class will continue in the covered car park.

2Reading · A Library for Things

阅读短文并完成 1–6。Short answer 题必须遵守词数要求。

A new “Library of Things” has opened beside the city library. Instead of books, members can borrow tools, kitchen equipment and camping items. A basic membership costs €12 a year, and most objects can be borrowed for three days.

Maya joined because she needed a drill to put up one shelf. Buying a drill for a single job seemed wasteful, so she reserved one online. At the library, a member of staff showed her how to use it safely. She returned it the next afternoon.

Some people worry that borrowed objects may be old or unreliable. In fact, every object is checked after it is returned. If it needs repair, the website marks it as unavailable until it is safe again.

The service is not completely free. Members leave a deposit for expensive objects. This money is returned when the object comes back on time and without damage. Late returns matter because another member may have reserved the same item.

The library helps people save money and reduce waste. It also lets them try equipment before deciding whether to buy it. However, it is not ideal for daily use. It works best for objects that people need only occasionally.

Questions 1–6
  1. What is the main purpose of the text?
  2. Why did Maya borrow a drill? Answer in no more than eight words. ______________________________
  3. Copy one sentence that challenges the idea that borrowed objects are unreliable. ______________________________
  4. What does deposit mean in paragraph 4?
  5. True or False: An object can still be borrowed while it is being repaired. ______
  6. Copy no more than six words showing that the service is unsuitable for something used every day. ______________________________
